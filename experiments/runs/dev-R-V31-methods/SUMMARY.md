## Why the early deck figure differs

The early comparison reported hit@10 on 24 cases over a 46-document corpus; the later hybrid (R0) hit@8 is on these 128 cases over 1,847 chunks of the current build. The two differ in three ways at once: (1) the cutoff, hit@10 against hit@8 (the table above gives both for the same cases, so the effect of k alone is visible); (2) the case pool, 24 early cases against the larger development set, which includes harder question families; (3) the corpus, 46 documents against the current build, where many more near-duplicate and period-confusable passages compete with the right one. The method is the same; the numbers are not comparable across those settings. This table is the like-for-like version.
