## What to say when something fails

- **Slow/network error:** “This live request failed operationally; that is not evidence that the statistic is unavailable. I will switch to the labelled recorded artifact.” Use the remaining slack once; do not repeatedly spend tokens.
- **Budget/busy/rate limit:** read the actual message, explain admission limits, and switch to recordings. Do not raise limits or expose keys on stage.
- **Unexpected answer:** read the status, inspect evidence and tool calls, and identify the unmet criterion. Do not edit gold labels or call a caveated answer an abstention.
- **Page/artifact mismatch:** stop using the stale headline and open the authoritative saved artifact. Static sections make no live model calls; a static page is not a live agent result.
- **Review/generalisation question:** “Human review is incomplete. Development and held-out results use draft AI labels and AI judging. The two registered held-out comparisons are inconclusive; they do not establish generalisation or a superior configuration.” See the speaking card above and `docs/concepts.md` for the practical trade-offs.
