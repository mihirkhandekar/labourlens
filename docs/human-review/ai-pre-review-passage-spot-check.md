**AI pre-review by claude-agent (ReviewSpotPassage)**

## Scope, method and limits

- Reviewed all **16 sampled rows** against local raw sources: 12 PDF passages from four reports and four HTML passages. Used PyMuPDF to read original PDF blocks and render each sampled block with surrounding content; used BeautifulSoup to inspect original HTML elements, line breaks, headings, superscripts and date elements. Did not call the application's extraction functions to establish fidelity.
- All **12/12 PDF samples** match a unique raw text block at their stored bounding box (coordinates agree within 0.11 PDF points); normalized block text matches exactly after whitespace normalization. Three HTML samples match individual original paragraph elements; the fourth matches an original line separated by HTML breaks. All **16/16** stored text hashes match the passage text. These checks establish local-source correspondence, not the completeness of every page or the semantic safety of an isolated block.
- PDF crops were visually inspected for missing words, column mixing, table structure and page furniture. All four table samples flatten two adjacent rows into one passage. No numerical transcription discrepancy was observed in those displayed rows, but headers, dates and units are outside each passage.
- Checked document identity against PDF cover quarter/year and HTML main headings. All sampled dates/stages are consistent with the corresponding local release pages. PDF publication dates were corroborated using the matching final-release HTML dates, **not** independently established from a publication-date statement inside each PDF. PDF creation/modification timestamps were inspected but are not treated as publication dates. No live-site/version comparison was performed.
- Actual passages are in `data/passages/*.jsonl`, not the assignment's suggested `data/processed/passages/`. Raw files are under `data/raw/<source_id>/<snapshot_id>/`. No raw files, extracted text, screenshots or long MOM quotations are reproduced here.

**Finding scale:** `ok` = sampled content is faithful and usable with its stated context; `minor` = text is substantially faithful but metadata/presentation contamination remains; `major` = material structural/context loss makes the isolated passage unsafe as a complete statement or numerical source. A major finding does not imply invented words or wrong numeric glyphs.

The four quarterly reports represented in the owner's sheet contain **3,726 passages**. Counts below are direct scans of local JSONL and, where specified, the raw PDFs. They are not retrieval-run results.
