# Frontend copy audit

Scope: all user-visible copy in `web/index.html`, `web/static/app.js`, `web/static/js/home.js`, `web/static/presentation/*.js` (not `evaluation.js` disclosures, which tests pin, and not workbench instrument labels beyond the pass 1 edits). Not changed: source quotations, model output, evidence paths, pinned disclosure strings.

## Pass 2: slide format (current)

Owner feedback: the copy read as LLM-written and did not read like a presentation. Rules applied:

- Every chapter, section and figure title is an action title: one sentence with the finding, its number and the sample size. Numbers are interpolated from `report.json` through the existing model code; none are typed in unless they were already literals in the file (6 tool calls, 2 rounds, 1 repair; 45 of 268; 125 cards).
- Under a chapter title: 2 to 4 terse bullets (what was tested, how, caveat). `chapter({lede})` was replaced by `chapter({bullets})`; `section(title, intro)` takes a bullet array or one short line.
- Figure `takeaway` kept only where it adds a decision or a limit; otherwise empty.
- First person for decisions, numbers first, limits next to the claim, "not demonstrated" where an interval reaches zero.
- New slides: title slide (home hero and write-up hero), Contents (10 chapters, with Work log as chapter 10), Summary (5 findings, 3 open items), Questions (6 questions with one-line answers from `docs/concepts.md`, `docs/evaluation-report.md`, ADR-002 and report values).
- Fixed time promises removed from the UI.

Approximate counts of strings rewritten in pass 2: 12 chapter titles, 11 chapter bullet sets (about 38 bullets), about 45 section titles, about 50 figure titles, about 45 takeaways reduced or emptied, 10 limit-card titles and texts, 6 next-step entries, 7 agent node descriptions and 8 trace explanations, 14 home-page strings, 12 title-slide strings. About 270 strings in total. New copy: Summary (8 lines), Questions (12 lines), Contents (10 labels).
