### Disposition vocabulary

- **RESOLVED-BY-AI-CONSENSUS**: no residual owner judgement is needed for a source-supported fact or agreed defect. Closure means the finding is established, **not that its implementation is fixed**. A pass not sampling an item is not a vote for it.
- **NEEDS-HUMAN-JUDGEMENT (J1–J10)**: a real rubric/methodology/acceptance choice, or publisher-side verification that the offline AI passes cannot establish.
- **Closed by primary evidence / historical correction**: an earlier AI diagnosis or historical label was wrong or superseded. This is explicitly **not unanimous original-pass consensus**. It would be dishonest to relabel an original disagreement as “all passes agreed.” The factual dispute is closed here, so it is not a residual judgement issue unless a J-number is attached.

This distinction is necessary: adjudication cannot both correct a mistaken AI pass and claim that the mistaken pass originally agreed. Tables identify those corrections rather than inflating consensus. No row below is human-reviewed.
