# Exploratory findings (generated from the validated store)

Numbers below are computed by `labourlens.data.eda` from `data/processed/observations.parquet`; they are descriptive, not causal.

1. **Vacancy-to-unemployed ratio** peaked at 2.55 in 2022-Q2 (since 2019) and stood at 1.48 in 2026-Q2, while the resident unemployment rate (SA) stayed between 2.6% and 2.9% across 2024-Q1 to 2026-Q2 (latest 2.9%). Figure: `artifacts/figures/fig1_vacancy_vs_unemployment.png`. The ratio is vacancies per unemployed person, not a percentage.
2. **Retrenchments** rose from 3,830 (2026-Q1) to 4,620 (2026-Q2), +20.6%; services were 77% of the latest quarter's top-level industry total. Figure: `artifacts/figures/fig2_retrenchment.png`.
3. **Revisions and definitions:** across 2025-Q3, 2025-Q4, 2026-Q1, 2026-Q2, final minus advance headline employment change was +300, -1,900, +4,400, +700 persons, and the sector-table total exceeded MOM's final headline by 5,100, 3,100, 3,600, 3,300 persons. The consistent gap is **consistent with** a migrant-domestic-worker definition difference but that is an inference, not a verified fact. Figure: `artifacts/figures/fig3_employment_change_reconciliation.png`.
